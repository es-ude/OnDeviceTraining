# The closures of har_train_step_roots.txt: over calls through pointers (its
# `indirect` line; exclude / site below), over the direct calls of its `self`
# roots (boundary below) and, as a cross-check, over what an instrumented step
# really runs (its `trace` line; trace-exclude at the end).
# Pointer closure:
# with it, --strict fails unless
#   - every function whose address the arm build takes (a vtable or conversion
#     cell in data, a kernel or callback pointer loaded from a literal pool) is
#     covered by the roots (named, or reached by a direct-call walk) or has an
#     `exclude` line here that says why it never runs on the bfp8full step; and
#   - every covered function that calls through a register has a `site` line
#     with exactly that many call sites and what they reach.
#   exclude <source.c> <function> <reason>
#   site    <source.c> <function> <indirect call sites> <targets>
# A new layer type, kernel arm, conversion cell or callback therefore fails the
# gate until it is rooted or excluded here; an entry that stops applying is
# stale. The exclusions were cross-checked against an instrumented host run of
# both heads (-finstrument-functions, the first three batches): none of them ran.
#
